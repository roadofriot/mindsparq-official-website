### 2. Executive Summary
MindSparQ Education & Technology is an education and technology organization focused on connecting foundational learning, practical skills, educational innovation, and digital technology.

The organization brings together traditional and modern approaches to learning, including Abacus Mental Math, Vedic Mathematics, handwriting development, STEM education, artificial intelligence, coding, robotics, teacher development, parent education, and digital learning solutions.

MindSparQ aims to help learners build knowledge, strengthen practical abilities, explore technology, develop problem-solving skills, and apply what they learn through structured activities and projects.

Its intended ecosystem extends beyond classroom instruction. It includes collaboration with schools, educator support, educational resources, digital learning platforms, software development, and AI-assisted educational tools.

The long-term direction is to develop a trusted education and technology brand originating in Nepal, with accessible learning opportunities and practical technology solutions for students, educators, families, and institutions.

### 5. Mission
MindSparQ's mission is to connect education and technology through accessible, practical, learner-centered programs and innovative digital solutions.
The organization seeks to:
1. Develop foundational mathematical understanding and mental calculation skills.
2. Encourage curiosity, logical reasoning, and problem-solving.
3. Introduce learners to coding, robotics, STEM, and AI through age-appropriate activities.
4. Support handwriting development and structured learning practice.
5. Help teachers integrate appropriate digital tools and modern teaching strategies.
6. Help parents participate constructively in children's learning.
7. Collaborate with schools to deliver educational programs and practical learning opportunities.
8. Develop digital platforms, learning resources, and software that address real educational needs.
9. Promote responsible, inclusive, and evidence-informed educational practices.

### 8. Educational Programs and Service Areas
*The organization is active, according to the founder's confirmation. The current operating status, timetable, availability, and delivery arrangements of each individual program should be maintained separately in the official program register.*

- **8.1 Abacus Mental Math:** Focuses on number sense, calculation practice, and the soroban abacus. UKG through Grade 5.
- **8.2 Vedic Mathematics:** Supplementary mathematics introducing alternative calculation strategies and number patterns.
- **8.3 Handwriting Improvement:** Developing clear, legible, consistent, and comfortable handwriting in English and Nepali (Print, Cursive, Mono, and Italic styles).
- **8.4 AI Coding and Robotics:** Practical technology learning introducing computational thinking, visual programming, Arduino UNO R4 WiFi & ESP32 robotics. Planned tiers: Robotics Explorer (Classes 3–4) and Young Robot Innovator (Classes 7–8).
- **8.5 STEM Education:** Connecting science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through design-and-build challenges and physical experimentation.
- **8.6 Teacher Training and Digital Education:** Professional educator development in learner-centered teaching, modern educational technology, and responsible AI-assisted preparation.
- **8.7 Parent Education and Student Development:** Constructive collaboration between families and educators, home practice habits, positive motivation, and student communication.
- **8.8 Additional Areas for Development:** Public speaking, leadership, school innovation clubs, advanced robotics, drone coding. DMIT-related fingerprint analysis treated strictly with scientific safeguards and not presented as fixed deterministic forecasts.
